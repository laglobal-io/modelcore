      1. Role of ModelCore

      1.1 Payment collection agent. Each Seller appoints ModelCore as its limited agent to receive payments from Buyers on the Seller's behalf. A Buyer's payment to ModelCore (through its payment processor) fully satisfies the Buyer's payment obligation to the Seller to the extent of the amount paid.       1.2 Payment processor. Payments are processed and held by Stripe, Inc. and its affiliates ("Stripe"). Each Buyer and Seller agrees to the Stripe Services Agreement and, for Sellers, the Stripe Connected Account Agreement. ModelCore may replace the processor on 30 days' notice.

      1.3 Not a party to the license. ModelCore is not a party to any Data License Agreement and does not deliver, host or inspect Licensed Data. ModelCore's decisions under these terms bind only the release or return of escrowed funds.

      2. Funding

      2.1 When. Within 5 business days after both parties accept an Order Form, the Buyer must fund the full License Fee, or the first milestone if the Order Form sets milestones, into escrow ("Funding").

      2.2 Methods. ACH debit from a bank account linked and verified through Stripe, card (Order Forms under $25,000), or wire transfer to the account ModelCore designates for that deal. ModelCore never asks for bank credentials by email.

      2.3 Failure to fund. If Funding does not arrive on time, the Seller may cancel the Order Form by notice through the platform with no liability. Samples already shared must be deleted under section 7.4.

      2.4 Clearing. Funds are treated as received only when Stripe reports them settled. ACH can take up to 5 business days to settle.

      3. Delivery

      3.1 Delivery obligation. The Seller's obligation to deliver starts on settled Funding. The Seller delivers the Licensed Data by the method and date in the Order Form directly to the Buyer. Licensed Data is never transmitted through or stored by ModelCore.

      3.2 Delivery Manifest. With each delivery the Seller uploads a Delivery Manifest to the deal record listing files, formats, record or hour counts, date ranges and a SHA-256 checksum per file. The Manifest is the record of what was delivered.

      3.3 Late delivery. If the Seller has not delivered within 10 business days after the Order Form delivery date, the Buyer may cancel and receive a full refund of escrowed funds.

      4. Inspection and acceptance

      4.1 Inspection Period. The Buyer has 14 days after the Seller marks delivery complete (or the period in the Order Form) to inspect the Licensed Data against the Specifications: the Order Form, the Listing as accepted, and the Delivery Manifest.

      4.2 Acceptance. The Buyer accepts by confirming delivery on the platform. If the Buyer does nothing before the Inspection Period ends, the delivery is deemed accepted.

      4.3 Nonconformity Notice. To reject, the Buyer must, within the Inspection Period, submit a Nonconformity Notice that identifies each material deviation from the Specifications with evidence (for example record counts, file checksums, sample rows, format errors or undisclosed personal data). Dissatisfaction with model results is not a nonconformity.

      4.4 Cure. The Seller has 10 business days after a Nonconformity Notice to cure by corrected delivery or by agreeing a price adjustment with the Buyer. A corrected delivery starts a new 7-day Inspection Period limited to the cured items.

      4.5 Partial acceptance. Where the Order Form sets milestones or separable parts, acceptance of one part releases the funds for that part.

      5. Release

      5.1 On acceptance or deemed acceptance, ModelCore instructs Stripe to transfer the License Fee, less the Platform Fee, to the Seller's connected account. Release is final and cannot be reversed by the Buyer.

      5.2 Release does not limit the Buyer's rights against the Seller under the Data License Agreement, including warranty and indemnity claims.

      6. Disputes over escrowed funds

      6.1 Escalation. If cure fails or the parties disagree, either may open a dispute on the platform. Both parties have 10 business days to submit statements and evidence.

      6.2 ModelCore review. ModelCore reviews the submissions and decides within 15 business days to release all funds, refund all funds, or split them. ModelCore may ask for more evidence or appoint an independent data reviewer, whose fee is shared equally unless ModelCore decides otherwise.
